# Asset notes

Manrope variable font: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope. SIL Open Font License, included in fonts/OFL.txt.

Noto Sans SC: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc. SIL Open Font License, included in fonts/NotoSansSC-OFL.txt. A local glyph subset is used for this website’s Chinese interface.

Phosphor regular icons: https://github.com/phosphor-icons/core. MIT license. Copyright Phosphor Icons. Used for navigation and links.

Portrait: extracted from the user supplied CV, without retouching.

Research figures and equipment photograph: extracted from the user supplied Academic Presentation PDF. Original scientific graphics are preserved. Source pages and editorial decisions are recorded in REDESIGN_V2.md outside the public site directory.

Third edition: the conceptual city SVG is reused as an accessible flat fallback. The 3D city is original procedural geometry and locally generated material textures. No source photographs or artwork from felixrieseberg.com are included.

Three.js is bundled locally for the 3D enhancement. Its MIT license is included as three-LICENSE.txt. The underlying static homepage works without external network requests.
